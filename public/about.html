About us

A local electrical contractor for homes and businesses.

We handle residential, commercial and industrial electrical work across Metro Vancouver.

Who we are

Triangle Electric Solutions Ltd is an electrical contractor serving homes and businesses in Surrey, Vancouver and the surrounding Lower Mainland.

Our work ranges from fixtures, chandeliers and EV chargers in homes to panels and power distribution in commercial and industrial settings. Whatever the size of the job, we aim to explain what is involved, do the work carefully and leave the area tidy.

If you are not sure what you need, call or send a message. We will talk it through with you and let you know the next step.
